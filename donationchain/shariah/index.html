Shariah governance

Shariah Compliance Board

Independent scholars review Zakat policy, asnaf eligibility, and disbursement rules. Every rupee of Zakat on this platform is routed only to verified institutions — never as cash to personal accounts.

Board members

Qualified scholars responsible for policy and case-level eligibility rulings.

Adopted rulings

Platform policy grounded in classical and contemporary Shariah guidance.

Compliance principles

  • Zero cash to personal accounts — Zakat and restricted funds pay hospitals, schools, pharmacies, and utilities directly.
  • Asnaf screening — cases tagged for Zakat must fit one of the eight categories (Quran 9:60).
  • Nisab + Hawl — calculator uses gold Nisab and a full lunar year; donors declare Hawl with an audit trail.
  • Certificates — Board can issue Shariah compliance certificates for Zakat distributions (CSR / personal records).
  • Transparency — invoices, delivery proofs, and ledger hashes remain available to the donor.

Staff / scholars

Case eligibility reviews and certificates are managed from Admin → Shariah Board.
