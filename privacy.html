Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to web and mobile apps

DonationChain connects donors and people in need. Payments go only to verified organizations (hospitals, schools, utilities). We minimize personal data on public pages.

1. Data we collect

  • Donors: name, mobile, optional email/ID, donation history, payment references (full card numbers are not stored on our servers).
  • Seekers (needy): application details, contact, documents for verification — stored for review, not shown on public case cards.
  • Technical: device tokens for notifications, basic logs, ledger hashes for transparency.

2. Khamosh (anonymous) donors

If you choose Anonymous / Khamosh donor, your name is not shown on public lists, shared receipts, or activity feeds. A private record may still exist for receipts, fraud checks, and legal compliance.

3. How we use data

  • Process donations and institutional payouts
  • Verify cases and reduce fraud
  • Send OTP, status SMS, and optional notifications
  • Zakat / Shariah compliance review where applicable
  • Platform security and audit trail

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with: payment providers (Raast, wallets, cards), verified vendor organizations for fulfilment, and staff/Shariah reviewers bound by access controls. Public explorer shows amounts and hashes — not seeker phone/CNIC.

5. Your choices

  • Donate anonymously
  • Request correction or deletion of account data via support (subject to legal retention for financial records)
  • Opt out of non-essential marketing SMS

6. Security

Passwords (staff) are hashed; user sessions use tokens; payment webhooks are signature-checked. No system is perfect — report issues to support.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: Contact page or email support@donationchain.pk
